Umamusume System Rules

By John Torena

Training

Training will go by biweekly segments where you can train one stat by first, rolling a check depending on the training on a training encounter table to see what happens.

Depending on the event, bonuses or reductions can be applied to the actual training roll of a d100 to see if the training went well. (A natural 100 on the training check grants double the total stat progress points)

If you succeed you progress training to a stat and lose progress if you fail, gaining a point to that stat when you reach max training progress.

  • Speed trains dex and str
  • Stamina trains con and wis
  • Power trains str and con
  • Guts trains wis, str, and dex
  • Wit trains int and dex and lowers your training DC by (10*your intelligence bonus)
  • Popularity trains cha and wis while also unlocking training perks

Each time you train you increase the dc of the next training check by 10 to a maximum of 100. Resting reduces the dc by 10 times 1d10 at the cost of a training slot and training wit reduces the dc by 10 if you succeed.

Every time you successfully train a stat you gain (2*Training level) stat progress points. Every 10 stat progress points equal a point into that stat. You also gain half of (2*Training level) stat progress points in that training’s side stats.

Your training level increases by 1 every 4 points you gain to an ability score from training that specific stat.

Training with your team will help you grow even faster

When you train with your team you all work together to grow stronger. To perform a team check you need to select a leader for the training roll and everyone will attempt to beat that player's training DC+10 for each additional person participating in the team check.

Everyone rolls an encounter check and adds the total together and the resulting effect affects everyone.

Next, everyone rolls their training check attempting to beat the leaders training DC. If they succeed they add +10 to the leader's training check result. If the leader succeeds the check them everyone gains stat progression equal to (2*Training level+1 for each additional person participating in the training).

Everyone participating loses energy, increasing their next training DC by 10 like a normal training. Additionally if you are doing team training with people you have levels of friendship with, the friendship mechanics will apply as normal, but the training DC reduction will only apply to the leaders friendship level.

Failing training and certain story events can cause you to suffer injury and have negative conditions affecting you.

  • Practice Poor: Suffer a -10 to training checks. You can either succeed on a dc 20 luck

check or spend a training in the infirmary to remove the condition.

  • Night Owl: Suffer a -10 to training checks. You can either spend a training in the

infirmary or roll a 7 or higher on a rest check to remove the condition.

  • Slow Metabolism: You cannot increase your speed stat in any way. You can either

spend a training in the infirmary or do 1d4 non speed stat trainings to remove the condition.

The infirmary is a place you can go to remove negative conditions at the expense of a training slot.

Succeeding on training and certain story events can cause you to gain perks and have positive conditions affecting you.

  • Practice Perfect: You gain a +10 to training checks. This can only be lost if you gain the

Practice Poor condition.

  • Fast Learner: Increases the chances of learning new skills when you train.
  • Hot Topic: You gain double the amount of fans.

Getting positive training events or reaching certain fan thresholds may also give you items that can help you train, race, or recover from fatigue or conditions.

  • Racing Notes: Acquire Fast Learner.
  • Tips For Efficient Training: Acquire Practice Perfect.
  • Cool Shades: Acquire Hot Topic.
  • Training Applications: Increase training level by 1, allowing for faster point gains.
  • Miracle Cure: Cure 1 negative status condition.
  • Artisan and Master cleat hammer: Gain double stat progress from racing.
  • Glow Sticks: Gain double fans from racing.
  • Good Luck Charm: Auto success on 1 training check.
  • Vita 20, 40, 60: Decreases the training check dc by 20, 40, and 60 accordingly.
  • Stat Ankle Weights: Doubles progress gained from training a certain stat but increases

the dc by 20.

Racing

On certain timeslots, races will be held where you can run in. If you are the only one racing, you can choose to just view the results of the race by rolling 1d20 and adding your highest ability score modifier to the result to beat a dc decided by the DM.

If multiple racers are skipping the race then the racer with the highest result that beats the dc wins.

If you are running the race in full, then you start by rolling initiative. Players go in initiative order and their goal is to win the race by reaching the finish line in the least amount of rounds.

During their turns, the players use the modified speed and dash mechanics as well as managing stamina and activating skills. They also have access to abilities granted by their class or feats.

You cannot activate skills within 10ft of the starting (Green) square.

Not meeting certain racing goals is only a setback for your character. Consider a failure as more information to try to achieve a different goal, or the same goal from another angle.

Completing a race will give you +2 stat progress to all of your stats, gaining an additional stat progress for if it's a G3 (3), G2 (4), or a G1 (5). You also gain additional stat progress if you get 3rd (+1), 2nd (+2), or 1st (+3).

Speed

Each racer’s base speed is 40 by default and can be increased by feats or class/subclass abilities.

Your maximum movement speed is 10 times your dexterity score. This is how fast you can run and must be built up to.

Power

When you take the Dash action, you expend 1 point of stamina and gain additional movement equal to 40 plus 5 times your strength modifier. (This is not d&d dashing, You only gain the additional movement not base speed +additional movement)

The additional speed gained from a dash lasts until the end of your next turn. If you fail to dash before the end of your next turn, you lose speed equal to 40 plus 5 times your strength mod each turn until you are at your base speed or you dash again.

This lets you either dash repeatedly to build up to your maximum speed, keep higher than base speeds without accelerating by dashing every other turn, or return to base speed after a few turns of deceleration.

Stamina

Each racer has a stamina pool equal to their Constitution modifier which is used to dash.

You can’t take the dash action if you are out of stamina unless you roll a (DC 10+Current Exhaustion Level) wisdom save to push past your limits and take a level of (2024) exhaustion

If you have exhaustion, the speed reduction applies to your dash bonus instead of your base movement (only for speed gained, not lost) and counts for the turn you gain the exhaustion.

If you gain a 6th level of exhaustion everything will play out the same as with exhaustion levels 1-5, but at the end of your turn you will fall unconscious and will be in critical condition for the next 6 training turns following the race.

During the 6 turns you will roll a d20, reducing the amount of turns you are in critical condition by 1 on an 11 or higher.

You regain your stamina on a long rest and half of your stamina on a short rest. (Minimum of 1) You also recover from all levels of exhaustion when you take a long rest.

Guts

Wisdom is the stat used to represent guts and is for will saves and tie breakers.

If two people cross the finish line in the same round they must roll wisdom checks and add it to their race initiative. The racer with the higher total number wins.

Wit & Skills

Intelligence is the stat used to represent WIt and is for skill activation. Skills are free action abilities gained through training and story events.

You may attempt to activate a number of skills each turn equal to your proficiency bonus by succeeding an intelligence check (DC 10 + Skill level).

Attempting to activate a skill consumes one use of that skill regardless of success or failure.

The same skill can only be used an amount of times equal to half your proficiency (rounded down, minimum 1) per race.

Each skill that doesn’t have an instant duration lasts a number of rounds equal to your proficiency bonus. Unless otherwise stated, the effect ends at the end of your turn on the final round of the duration. If a skill is currently in effect, using the skill again doesn’t stack.

Skills can influence speed, stamina, dashing, and mental awareness as well as granting racing maneuvers and are organized into levels (from 1-8) where higher level skills are more powerful and complex techniques, thus being more difficult to activate.

You start the game able to learn skills up to level 2. Being able to learn 2 additional levels of skills every 5 levels. (3-4 at level 6, 5-6 at level 11, and 7-8 at level 16)

Unique skills are stronger skills the players get to decide on for themselves that represent their racing style and personality and are unlocked by story progression.

You can only use one unique skill per race and must make a (DC 10) Intelligence check to activate it (the unlocking use is free)

Green skills are passive skills that automatically activate at the start of a race.

Skill Types

  • Speed Skills:
  • Increases your base speed
  • Increases your max speed
  • Decreases opponents base speed
  • Decreases opponents max speed
  • Dash Skills:
  • Increases speed gained from a dash
  • Reduces dash stamina costs
  • Decreases opponents' dash speed
  • Increases opponents' dash stamina costs
  • Stamina Skills:
  • Restore used stamina
  • Grant extra stamina
  • Manipulate opponents stamina levels
  • Maneuver Skills:
  • Improve Blocking and positioning
  • Improve Passing and overtaking
  • Mental skills:
  • Increase skill reliability
  • Resist debuffs
  • Overcome race obstacles

Winning Concerts

After a race you will need to perform in a concert by making a performance check to see how well you did.

You gain fans equal to 10 times the result with an additional 20. 40, and 60 fans for the top 3 racers.

Doing well in a concert can give you things like training perks and inspiration.
