Racers & Rivals Rules

By John Torena

Character Creation

When creating a new character, you gain 14 points to put into any stat, with no stat being able to go higher than 12. You also gain proficiency in any 3 hobbies of your choice.

Training

Training will take place in biweekly segments where you can train one stat by first, rolling a check depending on the training on a training encounter table to see what happens.

Depending on the event, bonuses or reductions can be applied to the actual training roll of a d100 to see if the training went well. (A natural 100 on the training check grants double the total stat progress points)

If you succeed, you progress training to a stat and lose progress if you fail, gaining a point to that stat when you reach max training progress.

Every time you successfully train a stat you gain (2*Training level) stat progress points. If you fail a training, you lose half as much. You also gain half of (2*Training level) stat progress points in that training’s side stats losing nothing if you fail. Every 10 stat progress points are equal to a point into that stat.

  • Speed trains Speed and Power
  • Stamina trains Stamina and Guts
  • Power trains Power and Stamina
  • Guts trains Guts, Power, and Speed
  • Wit trains Wit and Speed and lowers your training DC by 10
  • Popularity trains Popularity and Guts while also unlocking training perks

Each time you train you increase the dc of the next training check by 10 to a maximum of 100. Resting reduces the dc by 10 times 1d10 at the cost of a training slot and training Wit reduces the dc by 10 regardless of if you succeed or fail.

Your training level increases by 1 every 4 points you gain to an ability score from training that specific stat.

When you choose to train with a player or npc you can gain additional benefits depending on your friendship level, which is determined based on how close you are with them.

If you choose to do so, your training DC will increase by 20 instead of 10 after the training is completed. Friendship can be raised by training with a player or npc or just by spending time with them through roleplay.

If you choose to train with more than one player or npc, you all work together to grow stronger. To perform a team check you need to select a leader for the training roll, and everyone will attempt to beat that player's training DC+10 for each additional person participating in the team check.

Everyone rolls an encounter check and adds the total together and the resulting effect affects everyone.

Next, everyone rolls their training check attempting to beat the leaders training DC. If they succeed, they add +10 to the leader's training check result. If the leader succeeds in the check, then everyone gains stat progression equal to (2*Training level+1 for each additional person participating in the training).

Everyone participating loses energy, increasing their next training DC by 10 like normal training. Additionally, if you are doing team training with people you have levels of friendship with the friendship mechanics will apply as normal, but the training DC reduction will only apply to the leader’s friendship level.

The infirmary is a place where you can go to remove negative conditions at the expense of a training slot.

Negative Status Conditions

  • Practice Poor: Suffer a -10 to training checks. You can either succeed on a dc 20 luck check, gain the Practice Perfect condition, or spend a training turn in the infirmary to remove the condition.
  • Night Owl: Suffer a -10 to training checks. You can either spend a training turn in the infirmary or roll a 7 or higher on a rest check to remove the condition.
  • Slow Metabolism: You cannot increase your speed stat in any way. You can either spend a training turn in the infirmary or do 1d4 stat trainings to remove the condition.

Positive Status Conditions

  • Practice Perfect: You gain a +10 to training checks. This can only be lost if you gain the Practice Poor condition.
  • Fast Learner: Increases the Chances of learning new skills when you train.
  • Hot Topic: You gain double the number of fans.

Items

  • Racing Notes: Acquire Fast Learner.
  • Tips For Efficient Training: Acquire Practice Perfect.
  • Cool Shades: Acquire Hot Topic.
  • Training Applications: Increase training level by 1, allowing for faster point gains.
  • Miracle Cure: Cure 1 negative status condition.
  • Artisan and Master cleat hammer: Gain double stat progress from racing.
  • Glow Sticks: Gain double fans from racing.
  • Good Luck Charm: Auto success on 1 training check.
  • Vita 20, 40, 60: Decreases the training check dc by 20, 40, and 60 accordingly.
  • Stat Ankle Weights: Doubles progress gained from training a certain stat but increases the dc by 20.

Racing

On certain training turns, races will be held which you can run in. If you are the only one racing, you can choose to just view the results of the race by rolling 1d20 and adding your highest ability score modifier to the result to beat a dc decided by the DM.

If multiple racers are skipping the race, then the racer with the highest result that beats the dc wins.

If you are running the race in full, then you start by rolling initiative. Players go in initiative order, and their goal is to win the race by reaching the finish line in the least number of rounds.

During their turns, the players use the speed and dash mechanics as well as managing stamina and activating skills.

You cannot activate skills within 10ft of the starting (Green) square.

Not meeting certain racing goals is only a setback for your character. Consider failure as motivation to try to achieve a different goal, or the same goal from another angle.

Completing a race will give you +2 stat progress to all of your stats, gaining additional stat progress for if it's a G3 (3), G2 (4), or a G1 (5). You also gain additional stat progress if you get 3rd (+1), 2nd (+2), or 1st (+3) place in the race.

Speed

Each racer’s base speed is 40 by default and can be increased by certain skills.

Your maximum movement speed is 10 times your Speed score. This is how fast you can run and must be built up to.

Power

When you choose to Dash, you expend 1 point of stamina and gain additional movement equal to 40 plus 5 times your Power modifier.

The additional speed gained from a Dash lasts until the end of your next turn. If you fail to Dash before the end of your next turn, you lose speed equal to 40 plus 5 times your Power mod each turn until you are at your base speed or you Dash again.

This lets you either Dash repeatedly to build up to your maximum speed, keep higher than base speeds without accelerating by Dashing every other turn, or return to base speed after a few turns of deceleration.

Guts

If two people cross the finish line in the same round, they must roll Guts (Tiebreaker) checks and add it to their race initiatives. The racer with the higher total number wins.

Stamina

Each racer has a stamina pool equal to their Stamina modifier which is used to dash.

You can’t Dash if you are out of stamina unless you roll a (DC 10+Current Exhaustion Level) Guts Check to push past your limits and take a level of exhaustion.

If you have exhaustion, your Dash speed is reduced by 5ft and you have a -2 penalty to all d20 checks for every level of exhaustion you have (only for Dash speed gained, not lost) and counts for the turn you gain the exhaustion.

If you gain a 6th level of exhaustion everything will play out the same as with exhaustion levels 1-5, but at the end of your turn you will fall unconscious and will be in critical condition for the next 6 training turns following the race.

During the 6 turns you will roll a d20, reducing the amount of turns you are in critical condition by 1 on an 11 or higher.

You regain your stamina on a long rest and half of your stamina on a short rest. (Minimum of 1) You also recover from all levels of exhaustion when you take a long rest.

Wit & Skills

You may attempt to activate a number of skills each turn equal to your proficiency bonus by succeeding in a Wit check (DC 10 + Skill level).

Attempting to activate a skill consumes one use of that skill regardless of success or failure.

The same skill can only be used a number of times equal to half your proficiency (rounded down, minimum 1) per race.

Each skill that doesn’t have an instant duration lasts a number of rounds equal to your proficiency bonus. Unless otherwise stated, the effect ends at the end of your turn on the final round of the duration. If a skill is currently in effect, using the skill again doesn’t stack.

Skills can influence speed, stamina, dashing, and mental awareness as well as granting racing maneuvers and are organized into levels (from 1-8) where higher level skills are more powerful and complex techniques, thus being more difficult to activate.

You can learn skills via training or by dm discretion, with the maximum level of skills you’re able to learn being equal to your proficiency bonus.

Your proficiency bonus goes up every 50 total stat levels your character has.

Unique skills are Stronger skills the players get to decide on for themselves that represent their racing style and personality and are unlocked by story progression.

You can only use one unique skill per race and must make a (DC 10) Wit check to activate it (the unlocking use is free)

Green skills are passive skills that automatically activate at the start of a race.

Winning Concerts

After a race you will need to perform in a concert by making a performance check to see how well you did.

You gain fans equal to 10 times the result with an additional 20. 40, and 60 fans for the top 3 racers.

Doing well in a concert can give you things like training perks and inspiration.
